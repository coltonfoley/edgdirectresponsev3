## What The Page Must Prove

The Algonquin screen page should win on:

1. Local fit: Old Town, Main Street, Fox River, Randall Road, Algonquin Commons, High Hill Farms, Willoughby Farms, Lake in the Hills edge.
2. Buyer specificity: patio screens, porch screens, pergola screens, existing structure retrofits, and full outdoor room planning.
3. Comfort use cases: bugs, privacy, low sun, glare, wind, daily controls, and smart-home options.
4. Planning competence: opening measurement, track/headbox placement, fabric openness, power path, and service access.
5. Responsible permit language: screen retrofits may differ from pergolas, decks, and screen rooms; verify with Village Community Development when structure or deck scope is involved.
