## Repo and Funnel Mechanics

Source: local repo at commit `515da7e`, read-only inspection on 2026-07-02.

### Contact Routing and Context Preservation

- The contact page reads query parameters for `type`, `product` or `project`, `location`, and `area` or `market` (`src/components/features/contact/ContactClient.tsx:174-183`).
- It sets customer type, lead source, and lead market from those parameters, but only pre-fills submitted `projectType` and `location` when a `product/project` or explicit `location` exists (`src/components/features/contact/ContactClient.tsx:184-195`).
- `area` / `market` can change the UI market context, but it is not submitted as a structured location field unless the user types a location.
- The submit payload includes form data, `customerType`, and `source` (`src/components/features/contact/ContactClient.tsx:210-217`).
- Contact href scan found 117 contact links in TS/TSX files:
  - 50 plain `/contact`
  - 51 with `source`
  - 44 with `product`
  - 35 with `location`, `area`, or `market`
  - 40 with `type`

Implication: EDG has meaningful intent tagging on many CTAs, but there are still many generic contact paths and several paths where buyer context is not preserved as structured lead data.

### Form Friction and Lead Quality

- The contact form requires first name, last name, email, and zip/location in the browser. Phone, system interest, and project details are optional (`src/components/features/contact/ContactClient.tsx:332-449`).
- The server validates only email and first name before accepting a non-spam lead (`src/app/api/leads/route.ts:673-691`).
- The planning-guide capture form submits only first name, email, source, and honeypot (`src/components/features/contact/LeadCaptureForm.tsx:64-72`).
- The homepage hero form is more qualified: it requires email, zip, and project type in the browser (`src/components/features/home/HeroFormClient.tsx:89-145`).

Implication: friction is low, especially for guide capture, but Rainmaker receives uneven qualification context. That matches the live Rainmaker finding that `planning-guide-hero` has volume but often thin context.

### Lead Submission and Rainmaker Handoff

- The shared website lead hook posts to `/api/leads` and fires `generate_lead` only after a successful response (`src/hooks/useLeadSubmission.ts:57-81`).
- The website `/api/leads` forwards accepted Rainmaker leads with email, first name, last name, phone, location, project type, message, source, and customer type (`src/app/api/leads/route.ts:393-406`).
- The website does not forward structured metadata to Rainmaker in the current payload.
- Rainmaker's intake endpoint can accept `metadata`, and Rainmaker appends metadata to lead notes when present (`/Users/coltonfoley/Documents/Codex Projects/Rainmaker EDG Quoting/EDG-QUOTING/api/lead-intake.ts:18`, `:88-89`).
- Honeypot and content-spam matches return fake success without saving/sending a real lead (`src/app/api/leads/route.ts:666-670`, `:694-695`).

Implication: page URL, landing page, referrer, CTA label, form variant, query parameters, and configurator selections could be preserved in Rainmaker with low schema risk, but they are currently flattened or embedded only in free-text message fields.

### Configurator and Tracking Gap

- The pergola configurator posts directly to `/api/leads` with source `pergola-configurator` (`src/app/systems/pergolas/configure/ConfiguratorApp.tsx:566-579`).
- It does not use the shared `useLeadSubmission` hook that fires the `generate_lead` analytics event.
- Rainmaker has a recent qualified, quote-linked `pergola-configurator` lead, while GA4 shows 0 key events for `/systems/pergolas/configure` in the 28-day landing-page report.

Implication: the configurator may be commercially useful, but its current GA4 conversion tracking is incomplete. Do not judge configurator conversion rate from GA4 alone until this is fixed.

### Phone CTA Tracking

- Repo scan found 32 `tel:` href occurrences.
- Only 8 `TrackedPhoneLink` component usages and 1 `TrackedLink` tel usage were found.
- About 24 raw or framework `tel:` links appear not to use the tracked phone-click component.

Implication: GA4 `phone_click` undercounts website phone intent, especially on location, service-area, showroom, and commercial pages.
