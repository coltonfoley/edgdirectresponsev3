# Milwaukee motorized-pergola local SEO plan

Date: 2026-07-09

## Scope launched

- `/service-areas/milwaukee-wi` — Milwaukee outdoor-living hub
- `/service-areas/milwaukee-wi/motorized-pergolas` — high-intent product page
- `/service-areas/milwaukee-wi/zoning-guide` — source-backed permit and zoning guide

The cluster is linked from the Wisconsin regional hub, service-area directory,
pergola product page, navigation, footer, XML sitemap, and HTML sitemap.

## Search intent addressed

Primary intent is an installed, permanent motorized pergola in Milwaukee, not
a DIY kit search. The content addresses the questions that can prevent a lead
from moving forward: shade and rain control, screens, wind and privacy,
freestanding versus attached structure, drainage, lighting and heat, and the
City review path.

## Evidence and content boundary

Milwaukee has pages targeting the core query from local outdoor-living
competitors. EDG's opportunity is a complete, system-agnostic planning path
instead of a thin location substitution or a single-brand sales page.

The permit guide links only to City of Milwaukee sources and avoids publishing
unverified setbacks, fees, or permit determinations. The City determines the
requirements for the address and final project scope.

## Measurement plan

Track the new URLs in Search Console for impressions, clicks, CTR, and average
position against Milwaukee motorized-pergola, louvered-pergola, pergola
installer, permit, and related patio-cover terms. In Vercel Analytics and GTM,
compare Milwaukee-tagged fit-review starts and completed lead submissions with
other local pergola pages. Lead submission remains the existing website
`/api/leads` to Rainmaker flow; this SEO launch does not alter routing.

## Approval boundary

No Google Business Profile posts, profile edits, paid ads, citations, or other
public off-site actions are included in this launch. Requesting Google indexing
for the published URLs is part of the approved technical launch workflow.
