# Lake Forest Pergola SEO Plan

Date reviewed: 2026-06-23

## Why This Exists

Google Search Console showed a missing `/service-areas/lake-forest-il` URL while the broader Lake County page already positioned Lake Forest as a fit. Lake Forest is also a strong local-service opportunity because homeowners need permit-aware planning for permanent pergolas, exterior screens, heaters, lighting, and outdoor room systems.

## Pages Added

- `/service-areas/lake-forest-il`
- `/service-areas/lake-forest-il/motorized-pergolas`
- `/service-areas/lake-forest-il/zoning-guide`

## Messaging Guardrails

- Do not guarantee that every pergola requires the same permit path.
- Describe City rules as items to confirm before construction.
- Treat EDG as permit-aware planning support, not as the permitting authority.
- Keep the pages specific to Lake Forest architecture, mature lots, lakefront/wind/bug/privacy conditions, and City review, not generic location swaps.
