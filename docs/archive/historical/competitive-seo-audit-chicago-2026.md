## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

EDG Patio & Shade currently ranks well for several key terms but faces strong competition from established players. The Chicago outdoor living market is highly competitive with both specialized pergola installers and full-service landscape companies competing for visibility.

**Key Finding:** EDG appears in search results for most target keywords, often on page 1, but typically ranks #3-5 behind stronger competitors like Erdmann Outdoor Living and Warner's Decking who have more established domain authority and deeper content.

### TECHNICAL SEO RECOMMENDATIONS

#### 1. Schema Markup Implementation
Add LocalBusiness schema to all pages:
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "EDG Patio & Shade",
  "serviceArea": {
    "@type": "GeoCircle",
    "geoMidpoint": {
      "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
      "latitude": "42.3604",
      "longitude": "-87.9245"
    },
    "geoRadius": "50 miles"
  }
}
```

#### 2. Internal Linking Strategy
- Link from homepage to all service pages with exact match anchors
- Create topic clusters (Pergolas → Motorized Pergolas → StruXure Pergolas)
- Add breadcrumb navigation

#### 3. Page Speed Optimization
- Ensure sub-3-second load times
- Compress images (Next.js Image component already helps)
- Minimize JavaScript on above-fold content

## CONCLUSION

EDG Patio & Shade is well-positioned to compete in the Chicago market but needs to:

1. **Expand content depth** to match competitors (1000+ words per service page)
2. **Create dedicated landing pages** for each primary keyword
3. **Build local authority** through reviews and citations
4. **Target specific neighborhoods** with hyperlocal pages
5. **Leverage the system-agnostic advantage** vs. single-brand competitors

The Chicago market has room for a premium, design-focused outdoor living company that offers multiple brands and full-service capabilities. EDG's positioning as a "design-build partner" (not just a product showroom) is a key differentiator that should be emphasized in all content.

**Immediate Next Steps:**
1. Create dedicated service pages for top 4 missing keywords
2. Expand content on existing pages to 1000+ words
3. Launch review generation campaign
