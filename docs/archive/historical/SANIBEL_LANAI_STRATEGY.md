## Market Research Findings

### What is a Lanai? (Florida Definition)
- **Hawaiian origin** - means covered porch/patio
- **Required elements:** Covered roof + screen enclosure + ground level
- **Typical uses:** Poolside living, bug-free outdoor dining, transitional indoor-outdoor space
- **Standard construction:** Aluminum frame + fiberglass/screen panels + shingle or metal roof

### Current Competitive Landscape (Sanibel Area)

| Competitor | Type | Offering | Weakness |
|------------|------|----------|----------|
| **Fabri-Tech Screens** | Traditional Screen Enclosure | Pool cages, screen rooms, lanais | No motorized/adjustable options |
| **Ace Screen Repair** | Screen Repair/Rescreening | Repairs, replacements, gutters | Only repairs, no innovation |
| **Midas Screening** | Traditional Enclosure | Lanai enclosures, 25+ years exp | Static systems only |
| **Sanibel Screens** | Local Handyman | Screen repair, painting | Small local operator |
| **ScreenIT** | Pool Cage Specialist | 2-story cages, rescreening | No modern outdoor living |

**Critical Gap:** NOT ONE competitor offers motorized, adjustable, smart outdoor living systems.

### Lanai Cost Intelligence (2026)

| Lanai Type | Cost/SF | Total (200 SF) | Lifespan |
|------------|---------|----------------|----------|
| Screen only (existing roof) | $10-$25 | $2,000-$5,000 | 5-10 years |
| Full new lanai (screen + roof) | $50-$175 | $10,000-$35,000 | 10-20 years |
| Acrylic enclosure | $15-$40 | $3,000-$8,000 | 10-15 years |
| Glass enclosure | $30-$80+ | $6,000-$16,000 | 20+ years |
| **Louvered Pergola (EDG)** | $60-$120 | $12,000-$24,000 | 20+ years |

**ROI:** Lanai enclosures return 70-80% at resale in Florida markets.

## FAQ Strategy (Target Common Questions)

1. **"Can I replace my existing lanai with a louvered roof system?"**
   - Yes, and post-Hurricane Ian, many Sanibel homeowners are doing exactly this.

2. **"Does a louvered pergola count toward Sanibel's 40% impermeable surface limit?"**
   - When louvers are open, water passes through - often classified as permeable. This is a huge advantage over solid-roof lanais.

3. **"Is a louvered roof as hurricane-resistant as a traditional lanai?"**
   - More so. Our systems are Miami-Dade rated for 160+ mph winds - exceeding most traditional lanai construction.

4. **"Can I add motorized screens to a louvered pergola?"**
   - Yes. This gives you the best of both - the modern roof with optional insect protection.

5. **"What's the cost difference between rebuilding my lanai and installing a modern system?"**
   - Traditional lanai: $50-175/sf. Modern louvered: $60-120/sf. Over 20 years, the modern system often costs less due to zero maintenance.

6. **"Will a modern lanai look out of place in my Sanibel neighborhood?"**
   - Our systems are designed to complement coastal architecture. We offer white, bronze, and custom colors that blend with island aesthetics.
