# EDG Patio & Shade: Commercial & Hospitality SEO Competitive Audit

**Date:** February 2026  
**Focus:** Commercial/Hospitality Keywords (Chicago Market)  
**Auditor:** SEO Analysis Team

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

EDG Patio & Shade faces a fragmented but competitive B2B landscape for commercial and hospitality outdoor system keywords in the Chicago market. While EDG holds strong positions for several target keywords, there are significant opportunities to capture market share from national manufacturers and local design-build competitors through strategic content expansion and case study development.

### Key Findings at a Glance

| Metric | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Keywords in Top 3 | 3 of 8 |
| Primary Threat | National manufacturers (StruXure, Azenco, Roll-A-Cover) |
| Biggest Opportunity | Case study content and commercial project portfolios |
| Content Gap | Video case studies, interactive ROI calculators, downloadable B2B guides |

## 1. KEYWORD COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

### 1.1 "Restaurant Patio Enclosures Chicago"

| Position | Competitor | Type | Content Strategy |
|----------|------------|------|------------------|
| **#1** | **EDG Patio & Shade** | Local Installer | ROI-focused service page with revenue metrics |
| #2 | Roll-A-Cover | National Manufacturer | Product-focused with Chicago landing page |
| #3 | Enclosure Guy | National Supplier | Service area page with extensive product list |
| #4 | Cabrio Structures | Regional Manufacturer | Custom retractable enclosures page |

**Analysis:** EDG ranks #1 with a strong ROI-focused page. Roll-A-Cover is the primary threat - they have a dedicated Chicago page and emphasize year-round revenue generation. Their content focuses on glass retractable roofs vs. EDG's louvered systems.

**Differentiation Opportunity:** Roll-A-Cover focuses on glass enclosures; EDG should emphasize the louvered/pergola difference and local Chicago expertise.

## 2. TOP B2B COMPETITORS & THEIR STRATEGIES

### 2.1 National Manufacturers (Primary Threat)

#### **StruXure (Pergola X)**
- **Positioning:** Premium commercial louvered pergola systems
- **Content Strategy:** Extensive case study library (Del Frisco's, Moxie's, Lombardi's)
- **SEO Strengths:**
  - Dedicated case study section with real restaurant names
  - ROI-focused content with specific metrics
  - Commercial-specific product lines (Pivot 6 XL)
  - Strong visual documentation of installations
- **Keywords They Own:** "commercial louvered pergola," "restaurant patio ROI"
- **Threat Level:** HIGH - Best-in-class case study content

#### **Roll-A-Cover**
- **Positioning:** Retractable glass roof enclosures
- **Content Strategy:** Revenue protection messaging, "WOW factor"
- **SEO Strengths:**
  - Chicago-specific landing page
  - Featured jobs with restaurant names (Roots Pizza Chicago)
  - Strong differentiation (glass vs. louvers)
- **Keywords They Own:** "restaurant patio enclosures," "retractable roof Chicago"
- **Threat Level:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Direct Chicago competitor

#### **Azenco Outdoor**
- **Positioning:** European-designed, Florida-manufactured premium pergolas
- **Content Strategy:** Design-forward, R-Blade product focus
- **SEO Strengths:**
  - Restaurant profit-focused blog content
  - Specific ROI calculations ($2,000 per seat case study)
  - Premium brand association
- **Keywords They Own:** "restaurant pergola profits," "pergola manufacturer"
- **Threat Level:** MEDIUM - Growing presence, less Chicago-focused

### 2.2 Local Design-Build Competitors

#### **Chicago Roof Deck and Garden**
- **Positioning:** Full-service outdoor living (since 2009)
- **Content Strategy:** 7-step process emphasis, comprehensive services
- **SEO Strengths:**
  - Strong Yelp presence (313 photos, extensive reviews)
  - In-house professional team (architects, engineers)
  - Award-winning positioning
- **Keywords They Compete On:** "Chicago roof deck," "pergola design Chicago"
- **Threat Level:** HIGH - Established local player with full capabilities

#### **Reveal Design LLC**
- **Positioning:** Stylish, durable outdoor solutions
- **Content Strategy:** Feature-benefit breakdown, Google reviews integration
- **SEO Strengths:** Local neighborhood targeting
- **Threat Level:** LOW-MEDIUM - Smaller footprint, residential focus

#### **Botanical Concepts Chicago**
- **Positioning:** Architectural firm for outdoor spaces
- **Content Strategy:** Boutique, personalized approach
- **SEO Strengths:** 25+ years experience messaging
- **Threat Level:** LOW - More landscape-focused

### 2.3 Regional/National Installers

#### **Enclosure Guy**
- **Positioning:** Clear vinyl curtains and enclosures
- **Content Strategy:** Factory-direct pricing, extensive product lists
- **SEO Strengths:** Long-tail keyword coverage (clear curtains, windscreens)
- **Threat Level:** MEDIUM - Different product (curtains vs. hard structures)

#### **Cabrio Structures**
- **Positioning:** Custom retractable enclosures
- **Content Strategy:** Product configuration focus (multi-stack, single-stack)
- **SEO Strengths:** Technical detail pages
- **Threat Level:** LOW-MEDIUM - UK-based with US operations
