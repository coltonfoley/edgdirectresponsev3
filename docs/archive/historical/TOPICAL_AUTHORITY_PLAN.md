## Internal Linking Matrix

### Link Distribution Requirements

**From Product Pages:**
- Link to related guides: 2-3 links
- Link to pricing/quote: 1 link
- Link to relevant service areas: 2-3 links
- Link to gallery: 1 link

**From Guide Pages:**
- Link to pillar/hub page: 1 link (contextual)
- Link to related guides: 3-5 links
- Link to product pages: 2-3 links
- Link to contact/quote: 2 links (beginning and end)

**From Service Area Pages:**
- Link to all product types: 3 links
- Link to zoning guide: 1 link
- Link to projects: 1 link
- Link to pillar guides: 2-3 links
- Link to contact with area parameter: 2 links

**From Location Deep Pages:**
- Link to parent service area: 1 link (breadcrumb)
- Link to related location pages: 2-3 links
- Link to product page: 1 link
- Link to contact: 2 links

### Anchor Text Strategy

**For Pillar Pages:**
- Use exact match: "complete guide to louvered pergolas"
- Use branded: "EDG's pergola guide"
- Use partial match: "our comprehensive pergola resource"

**For Product Pages:**
- Use descriptive: "motorized louvered pergolas"
- Use action-oriented: "explore pergola systems"
- Use branded: "EDG pergola systems"

**For Service Areas:**
- Use location-specific: "Lake County pergola installation"
- Use service + location: "outdoor living in Northbrook"
- Avoid generic: "click here", "read more"

## Measurement & KPIs

### Topical Authority Metrics

**Domain-Level:**
- Total indexed pages (target: 150+)
- Pages per topic cluster (target: 10+)
- Internal links per page (target: 5-10)
- Average content depth (target: 1,200+ words)

**Cluster-Level:**
- Organic traffic per cluster (track month-over-month)
- Keyword rankings per cluster (target: 20+ keywords)
- Average position for cluster keywords (target: top 10)
- Conversion rate per cluster

**Page-Level:**
- Time on page (target: >3 minutes for guides)
- Pages per session (target: >2.5)
- Bounce rate (target: <60%)
- Scroll depth (target: >70%)

### Competitive Benchmarks

**Track Monthly:**
- Share of voice for primary keywords
- Ranking compared to top 3 competitors
- Content gap analysis (what do they have that we don't)
- Backlink gap (who links to them but not us)

- 50+ keywords in positions 1-3
- 200+ keywords in positions 4-10
- Top 3 rankings for "motorized pergola chicago"

## Risk Mitigation

### Duplicate Content Risks
- **Mitigation:** Ensure all location pages have unique local content
- **Check:** Use Copyscape or Siteliner monthly
- **Action:** Rewrite any pages with >30% duplicate content

### Thin Content Penalty Risk
- **Mitigation:** Maintain minimum 600 words per page
- **Check:** Quarterly content audit
- **Action:** Expand or merge thin pages

### Keyword Cannibalization Risk
- **Mitigation:** Follow hierarchy in this plan
- **Check:** Monthly SERP overlap report
- **Action:** Consolidate or differentiate competing pages

### Outdated Content Risk
- **Mitigation:** Implement quarterly update schedule
- **Check:** Review top 20 pages monthly
- **Action:** Update dates, pricing, regulations as needed
