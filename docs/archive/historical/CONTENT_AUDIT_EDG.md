# EDG Patio & Shade - Content & Keyword Strategy Audit
**Date:** February 2026  
**Auditor:** SEO Content Strategy Analysis  
**Scope:** Full site content audit with actionable recommendations

---

## Executive Summary

EDG Patio & Shade has a **solid content foundation** with good technical SEO implementation, but significant opportunities exist for expanding topical authority, resolving keyword cannibalization, and capturing more long-tail local traffic. The site has ~75 indexed pages with a strong focus on service areas and product pages.

**Overall Grade: B-**
- Technical SEO: A- (good schema, sitemaps, canonicals)
- Content Depth: C+ (thin content on many service area pages)
- Keyword Strategy: B (good targeting, some overlap issues)
- Topical Authority: C+ (missing pillar content and cluster depth)
- Internal Linking: B- (could be more strategic)

## 2. Keyword Cannibalization Analysis

### Identified Cannibalization Issues

#### Issue #1: "Motorized Pergola" Targeting Overlap
**Affected Pages:**
- `/systems/pergolas` (primary - ranks best)
- `/service-areas/northbrook-il/motorized-pergolas`
- `/service-areas/barrington-il/motorized-pergolas`
- `/service-areas/naperville-il/motorized-pergolas`
- `/service-areas/sanibel-outdoor-living/louvered-pergolas`
- `/service-areas/wilmette-il/louvered-pergolas`
- `/service-areas/winnetka-il/louvered-pergolas`

**Problem:** Multiple pages targeting "motorized pergola [location]" - diluting authority

**Solution:** 
- Keep `/systems/pergolas` as the primary "motorized pergola" rank target
- Location pages should target "[location] motorized pergola installation" or "[location] pergola contractor"
- Add canonical references from location deep pages to main product page for general terms

#### Issue #2: "Pergola Cost/Pricing" Overlap
**Affected Pages:**
- `/price` (general pricing page)
- `/guides/louvered-pergolas` (has pricing section)
- `/service-areas/*/motorized-pergolas` (mention pricing)

**Solution:**
- `/price` targets "pergola quote" / "outdoor living pricing"
- `/guides/louvered-pergolas` targets "how much does a pergola cost" / "pergola cost Chicago"
- Remove pricing focus from location deep pages

#### Issue #3: "Outdoor Living" Broad Term
**Affected Pages:**
- `/service-areas/lake-county-il`
- `/service-areas/north-shore-chicago`
- `/service-areas/sanibel-outdoor-living`
- Homepage

**Solution:**
- Homepage targets "outdoor living" + "motorized pergolas"
- Service areas target "[location] outdoor living" + specific products
- Add location modifiers to differentiate

#### Issue #4: Commercial vs Residential Overlap
**Affected Pages:**
- `/commercial` (general)
- `/commercial/hotel-pergolas`
- `/commercial/restaurant-patio-solutions`
- `/systems/pergolas` (could rank for commercial terms)

**Solution:**
- `/commercial/*` pages target "commercial pergola" / "restaurant patio systems"
- `/systems/pergolas` focuses on "residential pergola" / "home pergola"
- Add clear commercial/residential distinctions in content

### Keyword Cannibalization Map

```
Primary Keyword → Target Page → Supporting Pages
────────────────────────────────────────────────
"motorized pergola" → /systems/pergolas → All service area pages (differentiate)
"pergola cost" → /guides/louvered-pergolas → /price (link to, don't compete)
"[location] pergola" → /service-areas/[location] → Deep pages (more specific)
"commercial pergola" → /commercial → Industry-specific pages
"outdoor living [location]" → /service-areas/[location] → Location pages
```
