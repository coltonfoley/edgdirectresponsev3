# Competitive SEO Audit Report for EDG Patio & Shade
## Long-Tail & Informational Keywords Analysis
**Date:** February 2026  
**Prepared for:** EDG Patio & Shade Marketing Team

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This audit analyzes the competitive landscape for 10 high-value long-tail and informational keywords relevant to EDG Patio & Shade's service area (Chicago to Milwaukee corridor). The analysis reveals significant content opportunities, particularly in comparison content, local cost guides, and permit/zoning information where competitors have weak or incomplete coverage.

### Key Findings:
- **Low Competition for Local Chicago Content:** Most ranking content is from national publishers; local competitors have minimal SEO-optimized content
- **Featured Snippet Opportunities:** 7 of 10 keywords show featured snippet potential with structured content
- **Content Gap:** No competitor effectively covers "renson vs brustor" comparisons or Chicago-specific permit requirements
- **Directory Dominance:** Yelp, Houzz, and HomeAdvisor dominate "best company" searches - requires different strategy
